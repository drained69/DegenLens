# Sotto Pay

Sotto Pay makes requesting money from friends simple. Enter the amount you want to receive, generate a unique payment link or QR code, and share it with the people paying you. They can open the link or scan the QR code to make the payment directly to you.

Instead of exchanging account details or manually tracking who has paid, each request provides a clear, dedicated way to collect the money. It works well for splitting bills, collecting contributions, requesting repayments, or accepting payments in person.

The flow is straightforward:

1. Create a payment request and enter the amount.
2. Generate a unique link or QR code.
3. Share it with your friends or show them the QR code.
4. They complete the payment directly to you.
